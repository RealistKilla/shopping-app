## Brand & Style

This design system centers on a **Minimalist Corporate** aesthetic, tailored for a high-end mobile shopping experience. The brand personality is professional, reliable, and frictionless, prioritizing product imagery and pricing over decorative flourishes. 

## Colors

The palette is anchored by **Deep Indigo**, used strategically for primary actions, active states, and brand touchpoints. **Soft Slate** serves as the functional secondary color.

- **Primary (#3730a3):** High-intent actions (Buy Now, Checkout).
- **Secondary (#64748b):** Supporting information (Shipping details, SKU numbers).
- **Tertiary (#f8fafc):** Surface fills to distinguish sections from the pure white background.
- **Neutral (#1e293b):** Maximum legibility for body text and headers.
- **Background (#ffffff):** The base canvas for all views to ensure a clean, airy feel.

## Typography

This design system utilizes **Inter** for its neutral, systematic quality and exceptional legibility at small sizes.
